## Strong Subjects at New Zealand's Eight Universities

New Zealand's eight public universities each have their own areas of strength, including agriculture, tourism management, and film production. The entry requirements differ between undergraduate and postgraduate levels.

![A university campus in New Zealand](https://img.ulec.com.cn/留学-大学排名/unirank-io-guide-2026-08-20-3eb56b224e-image-01-2026-1880x1253.jpg)


### Agriculture

Several universities offer programs in agriculture, with a focus on practical skills and research. For undergraduate entry, applicants typically need to meet academic requirements such as secondary school qualifications and English language proficiency. Postgraduate programs often require a relevant bachelor's degree and sometimes prior work experience.

### Tourism Management

Tourism management is a key field, given New Zealand's landscape and industry. Undergraduate degrees generally accept students with high school credentials, while master's programs may require a related undergraduate degree and potentially professional experience.

### Film Production

Film production programs are available at some universities, combining creative and technical training. Entry to a bachelor's program usually demands a strong portfolio or audition in addition to academic scores. For a master's, a relevant undergraduate degree and portfolio are often necessary.

## Undergraduate vs. Postgraduate Entry Requirements

In general, undergraduate entry across these three fields requires completion of secondary education with satisfactory grades and proof of English ability. Postgraduate entry typically requires a bachelor's degree in a related discipline, and certain programs may ask for a portfolio, interview, or work history.

Because requirements vary by university and program, prospective students should verify specifics with the institution they are interested in.
