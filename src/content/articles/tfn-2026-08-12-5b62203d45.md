## Applying for a TFN and Filing Taxes

To work in Australia, you generally need a Tax File Number (TFN). You can apply online through the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) website. Once you have a TFN, your employer will use it to withhold tax from your pay. At the end of the financial year (30 June), you must lodge an annual tax return with the ATO, either online via myTax or through a registered tax agent. The specific procedures and deadlines for 2026 were not detailed in the provided information, so refer to the ATO for the latest guidance.
