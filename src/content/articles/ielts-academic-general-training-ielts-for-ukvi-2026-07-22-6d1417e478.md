## IELTS Test Types

IELTS test types include Academic and General Training. IELTS for UKVI and IELTS for UKVI Life Skills are also available. They have been designed to assess language ability that is more relevant for each specific sector.

## IELTS for UKVI and UK Visa Requirements

IELTS tests are accepted by the UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) as proof of English proficiency for those wishing to live, work, or study in the UK. This is the test to take if you are applying for certain types of UK visa.

## FAQ

### Q1: What are the different IELTS test types?

The IELTS test types are Academic and General Training. There is also IELTS for UKVI and IELTS for UKVI Life Skills.

### Q2: Which IELTS test is accepted for UK visa applications?

IELTS for UKVI is the test to take if you are applying for specific UK visa types, as it is accepted by UK Visas and Immigration as proof of English proficiency.
