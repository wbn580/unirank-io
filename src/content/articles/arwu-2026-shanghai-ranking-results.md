As of August 19, 2026, the Academic Ranking of World Universities (ARWU) 2026, published by ShanghaiRanking Consultancy, places Harvard University first with a total score of 100.0. Stanford University is second (80.2), and the Massachusetts Institute of Technology is third (74.1). This article explains how to read the ARWU 2026 results, based solely on the officially verified top 20 list and the ranking's published methodology.

## Who publishes ARWU and how it works

ARWU was first released in June 2003 by the Center for World-Class Universities at the Institute of Higher Education, Shanghai Jiao Tong University. Since 2009, it has been published and copyrighted by ShanghaiRanking Consultancy, an independent higher education information organization. ShanghaiRanking is not affiliated with any university or government department. The ranking is updated annually.

## The six objective indicators

ARWU uses six objective indicators to evaluate universities:
1. Number of alumni winning Nobel Prizes and Fields Medals
2. Number of staff winning Nobel Prizes and Fields Medals
3. Number of highly cited researchers selected by Clarivate
4. Number of papers published in Nature and Science
5. Number of papers indexed in the Science Citation Index-Expanded (SCIE) and the Social Sciences Citation Index (SSCI)
6. Per-capita academic performance relative to institutional size

These indicators are designed to measure research excellence and academic output using publicly verifiable data.

## Scale of the ranking

Each year, more than 2,500 universities are actually ranked. Of those, only the top 1,000 are published. This means that any university not appearing in the published top 1,000 was still evaluated but did not reach the publication threshold. The figures below reflect only the officially published 2026 list.

### How many universities are ranked and how many are published?

More than 2,500 universities are ranked each year, but only the top 1,000 are published. This means that many universities are evaluated but do not appear in the public list.

### What do the six indicators measure?

They measure Nobel and Fields Medal alumni and staff, Clarivate highly cited researchers, Nature and Science papers, SCIE and SSCI indexed papers, and per-capita academic performance. These indicators are objective and based on publicly verifiable data.
