The 2026 QS World University Rankings saw continued dominance by MIT (#1), followed by Imperial College London (#2) and Oxford (#3). The biggest story: Asian universities continued their decade-long climb.

## Top 20

MIT retains #1 for the 14th consecutive year. Imperial College London's #2 position (up from #6) reflects its strong citations and employer reputation scores. The top 20 remains dominated by US (8) and UK (5) institutions, with ETH Zurich, NUS Singapore, and Peking University representing Europe and Asia.

## Australia

7 Australian universities in the top 100: Melbourne (#13), Sydney (#18), UNSW (#19), ANU (#30), Monash (#37), Queensland (#40), UWA (#77). Australia has the highest number of top-100 universities per capita of any country.

## Asia Rising

Tsinghua (#14) and Peking (#20) both entered the top 20 for the first time. NUS (#8) remains Asia's highest-ranked university. The trend: Chinese, Singaporean, and Korean universities are gaining on US/UK incumbents, driven by research output growth and improving employer reputation.

## Methodology Changes

QS introduced Sustainability (5%) in 2024 and Employment Outcomes (5%) in 2023. These additions shifted rankings: universities with strong industry connections and sustainability programmes benefited. The core Academic Reputation weight was reduced from 40% to 30%.
