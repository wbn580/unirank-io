## The Australian Education Framework
Australia's education system includes universities, Technical and Further Education (TAFE) institutes, and private Vocational Education and Training (VET) providers. Each sector operates under distinct qualification structures and regulatory arrangements.

## Universities
Universities offer higher education degrees, including bachelor's, master's, and doctoral programs. They are self-accrediting institutions, meaning they have the authority to approve their own courses.

![Australian university campus](https://img.ulec.com.cn/留学-大学排名/tafe-vet-2026-08-18-32c04f9f75-image-01-2026-1880x1253.jpg)


## TAFE and Private VET
TAFE institutes and private VET providers deliver vocational qualifications, such as certificates, diplomas, and advanced diplomas. These qualifications focus on practical skills and industry-specific training.

Both TAFE and private VET providers are regulated by the Australian Skills Quality Authority (ASQA), which ensures national consistency in training quality.

## Qualification Pathways
Credits and articulation arrangements allow students to move between VET and higher education. For example, a diploma from a TAFE or private VET provider can often be used as a pathway into a university degree, with credit for prior learning.

## Choosing a Provider
When selecting a provider, students should consider the provider's registration, the quality of courses, and potential pathways to further study. The Australian government provides resources to check registered providers and accredited courses.
