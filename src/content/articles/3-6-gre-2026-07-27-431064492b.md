## Tailoring Your GRE Timeline to Your Starting Point

A three-to-six-month GRE preparation window allows candidates to build skills progressively. The exact length depends on the initial diagnostic score and the target score. A longer timeline suits those who need to strengthen foundational vocabulary and reading stamina, while a shorter, more intensive schedule works for test-takers already comfortable with advanced English. Regardless of the starting level, the plan should reserve the final weeks for full-length practice and review.

## Daily Task Breakdown for Verbal Focus

Daily study sessions should rotate through vocabulary building, text completion drills, and reading comprehension practice. For text completion, spend time analyzing sentence structure and common transition words before selecting answers. For reading comprehension, practice active reading by summarizing each paragraph in one sentence and identifying the author's main argument. Consistent, short daily sessions are more effective than occasional long study blocks. ## Common Questions About GRE Preparation

### How should I adjust my study plan if my diagnostic verbal score is below 150?

Begin with a three-month foundation phase focused on vocabulary and basic reading skills. Dedicate the first hour of each day to learning high-frequency GRE words and the second hour to untimed reading comprehension passages. Gradually introduce timed text completion exercises after the first month. ### What daily routine works best for improving reading comprehension speed?

Set aside 45 minutes daily for active reading of complex material, such as academic journal excerpts or opinion pieces. After reading, write a brief summary and answer inference questions without time pressure. Over several weeks, reduce the allowed time per passage. ### Can I effectively prepare for GRE verbal while working full-time?

Yes, by splitting study into two 45-minute sessions—one in the morning for vocabulary and one in the evening for practice questions. Use weekends for full-length sections and review.
