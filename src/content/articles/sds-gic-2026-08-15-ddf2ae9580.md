## What Is the SDS Stream?

The Student Direct Stream (SDS) is a dedicated study permit application channel for international students. It is available to residents of specific countries and is designed to expedite processing for those who meet the eligibility criteria.

## Updated 2026 Requirements

For 2026, the SDS stream maintains certain core requirements:

- **IELTS score**: Applicants must have an overall score of at least 6.0. This requirement applies to each band (listening, reading, writing, speaking) — no band can be below 6.0.
- **GIC amount**: The guaranteed investment certificate (GIC) required is CAD 20,635. This amount covers the first year’s living expenses.
- **Institution list**: The list of designated learning institutions (DLIs) eligible under SDS includes colleges, universities, and other post-secondary institutions that are recognized by the province or territory. The specific list is updated regularly; consult the official IRCC page for the current list.

## Advantages Over the Regular Stream

The SDS stream offers several advantages compared to the regular study permit application process:

![Documents for Canada study permit application](https://img.ulec.com.cn/留学-大学排名/sds-gic-2026-08-15-ddf2ae9580-image-01-2026-1880x1253.jpg)


- **Faster processing**: Applications submitted under SDS are typically processed more quickly than regular applications.
- **Simplified documentation**: SDS applicants are required to provide fewer supporting documents, as the upfront GIC and language test demonstrate financial capacity and language proficiency.

## How to Apply

To apply under SDS, you must:

1. Obtain a letter of acceptance from an eligible DLI.
2. Meet the IELTS requirement (6.0 overall, no band below 6.0).
3. Purchase a GIC of CAD 20,635.
4. Submit your application online through the IRCC portal along with the required documents.

Make sure to check the latest official IRCC guidance, as requirements can change.
