## Overview of AEIS and S-AEIS

The Admissions Exercise for International Students (AEIS) and its supplementary exercise (S-AEIS) are the primary avenues for international students seeking admission to Singapore government primary and secondary schools. These exams assess the students' English proficiency, mathematics, and reasoning abilities to determine eligibility for placement in the local school system.

## Exam Content and Format

AEIS and S-AEIS consist of two components: English and Mathematics. The English paper tests reading comprehension, vocabulary, grammar, and writing skills, while the Mathematics paper covers logical reasoning and problem-solving. The difficulty level is aligned with the corresponding grade level the student is applying for.


## Registration and Application Process

Registration for AEIS typically opens in July and closes in August, with the exam held in September or October. For S-AEIS, registration takes place in January and the exam is conducted in February or March. Parents or guardians must submit an online application through the Ministry of Education's website, including the child's particulars, passport details, and required documents. A non-refundable application fee is applicable.

## Preparation Resources

To prepare for these exams, students can use official practice papers and past exam questions available from the Ministry of Education. Additionally, many educational institutions and online platforms offer preparatory courses and materials tailored to the AEIS and S-AEIS syllabus. It is advisable to start preparation early and focus on strengthening English language skills, as this is often the main challenge for international students.

## Difficulty and Challenges

The competition for admission to Singapore government schools is intense, as there are limited vacancies for international students. Besides the exam itself, students must adapt to the local curriculum and language environment. The overall difficulty depends on the student's current academic level, language proficiency, and the year of entry. Success requires consistent effort and thorough preparation.

## Final Thoughts

International students and their families should plan well in advance, understand the requirements, and seek appropriate guidance to navigate the admission process. With adequate preparation, students can improve their chances of securing a place in a Singapore government school.
