When choosing an Australian study abroad agency, the first step is to separate brand recognition from verifiable service scope. A well-known name does not replace a written agreement that states which universities, courses, and services are included. The comparison below ranks agencies by category and score, but these scores are comparison aids, not official ratings or guarantees. Before signing anything, confirm the agency's current service boundaries, fees, and delivery steps in writing.

## Why Category Matters More Than a Single Score

A single score cannot tell you whether an agency handles your specific situation. Different categories serve different needs. A global agency may have broad reach but less local knowledge. A domestic chain may offer Chinese-language support but unclear offline coverage. A local specialist may know one city well but not others. Platforms and free tools can help you start, but they do not replace professional advice. The right choice depends on your target level, budget, and whether you need help beyond application submission.

## Global Agencies

Compare global agencies when you want a single provider that can handle multiple countries or when you are still deciding between Australia and other destinations. These agencies often have multilingual teams and broader institutional knowledge. However, their service delivery may vary by office or team. Before choosing one, verify in writing which countries, universities, and stages of the application process are included. Ask whether the fee covers initial consultation, document review, application submission, and follow-up communication with the university.

## FAQ

### How do I verify if an agency is officially recognized by an Australian university?

Australian universities are required to record their partner education agents in the PRISMS system under the ESOS framework. However, PRISMS is not a public portal for students to search for agents. The recommended approach is to check the university's official website for its list of partner agents and confirm the agency's name appears there.

### What should I ask an agency before signing a contract?

Ask for a written breakdown of all fees and the specific services included. Study Australia advises comparing service fees across different agencies and clarifying what is covered. Also ask whether the agency is paid by the institution, as this cost may already be included in your tuition. Confirm in writing which universities and courses are covered and whether follow-up communication with the university is included.

### Can an education agent provide immigration advice?

Education agents can help compare courses and process applications, but they usually cannot provide regulated immigration legal advice. If you need visa or migration advice, you should consult a registered migration agent or lawyer. The agency's role is typically limited to application support, not legal counsel.
