## Understanding the ID995A Form

The ID995A form is a required document for Hong Kong student visa applications. It captures your personal information, proposed study details, and financial situation. When completing the form, ensure that all sections are filled accurately to avoid delays.

## Step-by-Step Completion

### Personal Information

Provide your full name, date of birth, and passport details exactly as they appear in your passport. Also include your contact details and your intended address in Hong Kong.

### Proposed Study Details

Indicate the name of the institution and the course you have been admitted to. Include the expected duration of stay and the date you plan to arrive in Hong Kong.

### Financial Information

The form asks for your expected financial support, including the source of funds and amount available. Be prepared to provide supporting documents that verify your financial capacity.

## Supporting Documents

### Financial Proof

You will need to provide bank statements or other evidence that demonstrates you have sufficient funds to cover tuition and living expenses during your stay. The source of these funds must be clearly documented.

### Accommodation Arrangements

Include details of your accommodation in Hong Kong, such as a hostel, university housing, or private rental. Provide a contract or confirmation letter as proof of your housing arrangements.

### Additional Documents

Other supporting documents may include your admission letter, academic transcripts, passport copies, and recent photographs. Check with the consulate or the Immigration Department for the specific requirements.

## Important Reminders

Double-check all entries on the ID995A form for accuracy before submission. Ensure that all supporting documents are complete and clearly legible. Submit the application well in advance of your intended start date to allow for processing time.
